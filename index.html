The Mexico City Grand Prix, a shared 3D world

A live 3D circuit you can walk through as a visitor. Everyone who opens this artifact joins the same paddock: you see each other’s avatars, chat, react and watch one synchronised race, scripted from the official race reconstruction, with a two-person commentary booth. Voice-over uses your browser’s speech engine.

Commentary booth

Multiplayer works for people signed in to claude.ai who have access to this artifact; anyone else explores solo. The circuit and grandstands are a stylised model, not a survey. Race events follow the supplied guide; where it records no distinct event, the booth stays quiet instead of inventing one. The guide shows the final Safety Car restart on both laps 65 and 71; this sim runs the field behind the Safety Car until the final-lap restart.
